## McDonald's ends IBM drive-thru AI test (June 2024)
- McDonald's ended its two-year automated order taking test with IBM and removed it from more than 100 restaurants; shut off no later than July 26, 2024.
- Viral videos showed errors such as adding nine sweet teas instead of one, and butter and ketchup packets added to ice cream orders.
- McDonald's said voice ordering "will be part of our restaurants' future".

## Taco Bell slows AI drive-thru rollout after "18,000 waters" (2025)
- After rolling voice AI to about 500 drive-thrus, Taco Bell reassessed following viral glitches and trolling.
- A customer ordered 18,000 cups of water to overwhelm the system so he could speak to a human.
- Taco Bell said the AI had handled over two million orders; it is teaching staff when to take over.
